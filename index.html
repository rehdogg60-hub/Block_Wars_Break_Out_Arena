How To Play

  • Desktop: move with WASD or arrow keys. Aim with the mouse. Click or press Space to fire.
  • Mobile: use the directional pad and Fire button.
  • Hide behind crates and barriers. Clear each wave to score.
  • Press P or Escape to pause. Sound files are optional.
